About the chef

Personal, polished, and quietly memorable.

Having completed my apprenticeship through the Royal Academy of Culinary Arts at 3 michelin starred restaurant, Core by Clare Smyth, it has allowed me to develop a strong foundation in classical and contemporary fine dining. Throughout my career, I have also contributed to a range of prestigious culinary events and projects, including representing Team UK on the international stage at the Bocuse d'Or, one of the world's most respected culinary competitions.

Services

How to book an experience.

01

Define Your Vision

Determine your guest count, preferred date, and culinary preferences. Should you have any questions or require initial guidance, please feel free to call or email.

02

Request a Custom Proposal

Submit your event details via email to receive a tailored menu and a comprehensive quote designed specifically for your occasion.

03

Confirm the Date and Details

Secure your booking with a deposit. At this stage, we can schedule an optional consultation or tasting session to finalize the details and meet in person.

04

Relax and enjoy

On the day of your booking, all grocery sourcing, preparation, execution, and kitchen clean-up will be managed seamlessly so you can focus entirely on your guests.
